Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Claude Tasks is a personal tool used only by its owner, Adonias Nigussie.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads, creates, edits, completes, and deletes tasks in the owner's Google Tasks account (scope: Google Tasks).

Where data goes

The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Task data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from any other person is collected.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the contact links on adoniascodes.github.io.
